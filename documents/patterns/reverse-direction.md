# Reverse Direction

## Problem
Chat feels like talking to a person, so you behave as you would with one: AI asks, you answer; or it sits there taking orders, so you hand it another order.
Every answer cements you as the answerer, every order as the order-giver; the conversation gathers inertia, locking you into whichever role you started in.

But AI isn't leading — it's trained to follow you.
Follow the follower, and nobody is driving: the conversation drifts somewhere neither of you chose, instead of where you need to go.

## Pattern
Don't let AI steer you. 
You hold the steering wheel at every turn; you don't owe the conversation its expected next move.

Catch yourself just before you respond:

* AI asks you a question — before answering, consider asking for its input instead: *"Suggest some options"* or *"What would be good here?"*
* You're about to tell it how to do something — you've been working alone, and there may be alternatives you haven't considered: *"What different approaches could we take?"*

That second reversal is also an instrument of vision.
You stand at a crossroads seeing just one or two paths ahead; AI can show you the ones you can't see, the ones you don't know you don't know.
It's easier to see something and know you don't like it than to specify the exact thing you want right away ("it's easier to complain than to explain," Gojko Adžić), and seeing several options gives you more ideas of your own.

## Example

**File renaming:**
- You: "Rename this file"
- AI: "What should I rename it to?"
- You reverse: "Can you think of some good names?"
- AI shows options with reasoning.
- Seeing them gives you more ideas, shows you quickly what you don't like, or lets you combine the best bits from several.
